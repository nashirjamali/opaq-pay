# antislop audit 001, app design (2026-10-11)

Mode: after. Scope: the signed-in app shell and its screens in `apps/web` (Overview, Receive, Activity, Cash out, Reports, Settings, dialogs, the payer page), light and dark, desktop and 390px. The marketing landing page was audited separately. Direction: `DESIGN.md` (ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2, Coinbank style, rich style section).

Method: read the code, scanned computed text contrast on six screens in both themes (desktop), scanned tap targets on seven screens at 390px, grepped for em dashes, buzzwords, outline removal and stray icon sets. Not done in this audit: a full manual click-through of every control (R-35).

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## Findings

1. **HIGH, R-25 (contrast). FIXED.** Muted text is now `#666679` (about 5.2:1 on the tint); rescanned light and dark, no failures. The network pill ("Solana Devnet", muted `#717184` on `#F4F4F6`) measures 4.35:1, under the 4.5:1 AA minimum for 12.5px text. It shows in the header on every screen in light mode. The "What your payer sees" tag on Receive has the same pair at 4.35:1. Fix: use the ink color or a darker muted (`#6A6A7C` or darker) on `--surface2`.
2. **HIGH, R-03 (44px tap targets). FIXED.** At 1099px and below, small buttons, the header logo, "Hide amounts" and the text links get 44px targets; rescanned at 390px, none under 44px. At 390px these are under 44px: header and banner "Create account" (39px, `btn-sm`), the header logo link (38px), "Hide amounts" (40px), "Ask for a specific amount" (36px), "See all activity" (36px). Fix: raise `btn-sm` to 44px on small screens, pad the logo link, and give the text links a 44px hit area.
3. **HIGH, R-24 (navigation). FIXED.** Cash out and Reports carry a "Soon" tag in the sidebar and the drawer. Cash out and Reports are in the sidebar and the mobile drawer with no "Coming soon" label, but both pages are "coming soon" placeholders. The page itself is honest; the nav is not. Fix: add a small "Soon" tag to those two items, or hide them until built.
4. **MEDIUM, R-32 (keyboard, mobile drawer).** When the menu is open, focus moves into it, but focus is not trapped and does not return to the menu button on close, and the page behind stays tabbable. Fix: trap focus while open (or use the `inert` attribute on the rest of the page) and restore focus to the button on close.
5. **MEDIUM, R-26 / C-2 (dead end).** In the signed-out demo, the "payment link" (`.../pay/demo`) opens a page that says "No one is registered as @demo". It is sample data, but the link is shown as something that works. Fix: label it as a sample link ("Create an account to get a working link"), or make `/pay/demo` show the example payer page.
6. **LOW, C-3 (redundancy).** On Overview, the "Waiting to move" stat repeats what the balance card already says ("120.00 USDC arrived and is not in your private balance yet"). Fix: drop the stat, or turn it into the one place that shows the count and shorten the card text.
7. **LOW, R-06 (typography).** Stat labels ("PRIVATE PAYMENTS", "WAITING TO MOVE") use uppercase with wide tracking at 12px. Fix: sentence case at 13px, or write down why the uppercase helps scanning.
8. **LOW, R-30 (clone), owner-directed.** The app follows the Coinbank design system by your request. No action; recorded as an override.

## Passed (with evidence)

- R-02: no em dashes in `app`, `components`, `lib`.
- R-16: no buzzwords found by grep.
- R-17/R-18/R-36/R-38: demo data is labelled by the banner ("This is a demo with sample data"); no testimonials, stats or compliance claims.
- R-04: one icon set (Coinbank); the only others are the theme moon/sun and the Google mark.
- R-12/R-13/R-10: shadow only on dialogs; no glow; no glass.
- R-25 in dark mode: no contrast failures on six screens.
- R-27: loading, error and empty states exist (`DataStates`, empty cards, activity filters).
- R-32: dialogs are native `<dialog>` (Escape and focus handling built in); the only `outline: none` on inputs is replaced by a visible 3px ring.
- R-34: light and dark both verified.
- R-37/R-31: direction and reasons recorded in `DESIGN.md`.
